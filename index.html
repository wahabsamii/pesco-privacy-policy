Privacy Policy

PESCO Bill Calculator

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PESCO Bill Calculator. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our mobile application.

PESCO Bill Calculator is designed to help users estimate their electricity bills based on consumed electricity units. We aim to keep your information private and provide a transparent user experience.

2. Information We Collect

PESCO Bill Calculator does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features.

The application may process the following information:

  • Electricity Units: The number of electricity units entered by users to calculate an estimated electricity bill.
  • Saved Bill History: Previously calculated bills that users choose to save.
  • Language Preferences: Your selected application language (English or Urdu).

This information is stored locally on your device for application functionality.

3. How We Use Information

The information processed by the application is used for:

  • Calculating estimated PESCO electricity bills.
  • Displaying previously saved bill calculations.
  • Remembering your preferred application language.
  • Improving the application's functionality and user experience.

4. Data Storage and Security

Saved bill history and language preferences are stored locally on your device using device storage.

We do not operate a personal user account system or a server that receives your bill calculation history.

You can remove locally saved application data by deleting saved records or uninstalling the application.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

If third-party services, such as advertising providers, are integrated into the application, those services may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services in the future, including advertising or analytics services.

Such services may collect device-related information or other data as described in their respective privacy policies.

Users should review the privacy policies of any third-party services integrated into the application.

7. Children's Privacy

PESCO Bill Calculator is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application's core functionality.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can:

  • Choose whether to save your bill calculations.
  • Delete saved bill history.
  • Change your preferred application language.
  • Uninstall the application to remove its locally stored application data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
